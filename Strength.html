Good to know

Questions before you begin.

Do I need experience with barbells?

No. This is a beginner course, and the movements are introduced with coaching and options for your starting level.

What if I have not exercised in a while?

The starting-block guide helps you reflect on your experience and support preferences. A coach can discuss the programme with you before you decide.

Is a DEXA scan a diagnosis?

No. A DEXA scan is a measurement; it does not diagnose a health condition. Ask your clinician about medical questions or whether a scan is appropriate for you.

What if I have an injury or health condition?

Please speak with your healthcare professional and the programme team before taking part. This short qualifier does not assess medical readiness.

Can I help a parent decide?

Yes. You can complete the guide together, but the answers should reflect the participant’s own goals, comfort and preferences.

How much does the programme cost?

The confirmed programme price and whether the DEXA scan is included will be shown before you book.
